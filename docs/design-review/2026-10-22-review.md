# Design review: ShelfSprite, 2026-10-22

Run of the brief in [2026-10-22-brief.md](2026-10-22-brief.md) with impeccable 4.7.0 (`critique`,
then `audit`). The standard was ShelfSprite's own approved identity: Ink & Paper's rail and
reading-first layout with Ember & Ivory's warm palette, dark only. Fixes that keep that identity
were made; anything that would change it is listed under [Proposals](#proposals-yesno) and was not
coded.

Screenshots are under [`2026-10-22/`](2026-10-22/), all at device scale 1: `before/` and `after/`
(1280 × 800 `-desktop`, 390 × 844 `-phone`, full-page unless a modal is open), `states/` (empty
database and database-down captures) and `evidence/`. In full-page phone captures the fixed
BottomNav appears partway down the image; that is a capture artifact, not a layout bug.

## Read first

- **Book covers did not load.** `covers.openlibrary.org` redirects to `archive.org`, which this
  environment's network proxy refuses (`ERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED`). Every cover in every
  capture is the app's neutral book-icon fallback, so cover-dependent layout was judged from the
  fallback. The fallback held up well in Library and Home rows; it was broken in three places
  (fixed, see F8).
- **Fonts loaded.** `next/font/google` fetched Bricolage Grotesque, Inter and JetBrains Mono;
  `document.fonts.check` was true for all three on every page.
- **Not reachable honestly:** a populated swipe deck (needs a paid recommendation run), Discover
  results and Similar reads (paid Claude calls), and the database-down error branch on `/swipe`
  (see N-S1: the API hangs instead of failing). These were judged from source.
- **impeccable ran unattended.** Its context step asks for one structured question before working
  autonomously; Chase's instruction for this run was that he is not watching, and the brief settles
  direction, so no question was asked. For the same reason each critique ends with
  `Questions skipped: brief settles direction; owner unavailable`. Critique snapshots were not
  persisted to `.impeccable/critique/` because that path is outside the brief's allowed files.

## Method

- Critique ran as impeccable requires: Assessment A (design review) in two isolated sub-agents,
  one for the entry surfaces and reveal, one for the app surfaces, and Assessment B (detector and
  browser evidence) in a third, isolated from A until both finished. Method:
  `dual-agent (A1, A2 · B)`.
- Assessment B ran `impeccable detect --json app components`, injected impeccable's overlay
  (`detect.js` via its live server) into 9 routes at both widths in headless Chromium, and
  measured contrast for 40 colour pairs, font sizes, touch targets, keyboard focus on every
  tabbable element, reduced-motion behaviour, font loading and overflow.
- Modes: `/welcome` Persuade; the reveal Experience; everything else Operate.
- Local mode, the fictional 19-book demo library from `scripts/demo-library.sql`, user `local`
  (admin). Empty states: the same database migrated but not seeded. Error states: PostgreSQL
  stopped while the app ran. Both were restored afterwards; no code was left behind to fake a state.

**Implementation integrity verdict: pass.** The shell, Home, Read shelf, Profile and reveal are
specific to ShelfSprite and could not be reused unchanged by another product. Drift is concentrated
in controls written by hand instead of through `components/ui` (inputs with `border-border` and
`ring-1`), which is where most of the accessibility fixes landed.

### Detector

- CLI: 7 findings. 6 `broken-image` are in `__tests__` files mocking `next/image` (false positives).
  1 `side-tab` at `app/(marketing)/welcome/page.tsx:112` is the illustrated book spine (identity;
  proposal P6).
- Overlay: per-page counts from 0 (`/swipe` desktop) to 24 (`/welcome` phone). Real: 10px
  `.eyebrow` text and a kicker above every app-page heading (identity; P1), 11px text inside the
  decorative `ResolveArtifact`, cramped trait pills on phone profile, nested cards in the Danger
  zone. Judged false positives: nested cards on the welcome spines and the cover placeholder,
  `overused-font` (Inter body is the approved pairing).
- After fixes, `impeccable detect --json` over every changed `.tsx` file: **0 findings**.

### Measured

- Text contrast: no failures among 40 pairs. Lowest readable pairs: faint on elevated 5.68:1,
  danger chip 4.65:1, accent on elevated 5.49:1, dark ink on accent 6.98:1.
- Below threshold: control edges `--border` 1.14–1.45:1 (fixed, F3); inactive reveal progress dots
  1.27:1 (fixed, F1); disabled primary button 1.72:1 (exempt under 1.4.3; proposal P12).
- Fonts load; no horizontal overflow at 390px on any page; `tabular-nums` is unused, but every
  aligned number is in JetBrains Mono, so columns do not jitter (no finding).

## Proposals (yes/no)

Not coded, because each would change identity.

1. **Eyebrows:** raise `.eyebrow` from 10px uppercase to 12–13px (keeping the uppercase tracking), or move to sentence case? (`app/globals.css:200`; every page-heading kicker, "YOUR COLLECTION".)
2. **Rail label:** "YOUR READING ROOM" in the rail from 11px uppercase to 13px sentence case? (`NavBar.tsx:101`)
3. **Mono micro-labels:** set "YOUR RATING", "FIX MATCHES · 1 OF 1", the swipe card's "About / Why for you / Matched taste", "READER TYPE" and the 10px poster-tile eyebrow in 13px sentence case?
4. **Welcome motion:** play `ResolveArtifact`'s rise/draw sequence when it scrolls into view? It runs once at page load about 2,400px down (3,043px on phone), so no visitor sees it play. The composed end state stays the default.
5. **Welcome hero:** move `ResolveArtifact`, the page's one proof only ShelfSprite can show, into the hero in place of the illustrative three-book shelf?
6. **Welcome spines:** drop the `border-l-4` binding edge on the three hero spines (the detector's side-stripe finding), or keep it as part of the illustration?
7. **Reveal aversions:** drop the `border-l-2 border-danger/40` stripe on aversion items (`TraitBeats.tsx:211`)?
8. **Reveal trait beats:** add a quiet "Next" for "not sure" and a Back control, instead of requiring a verdict on every trait?
9. **Home heading:** make "Find a book that stays with you." the H1, and demote "Welcome back." (36px H1 under a 60px H2)?
10. **Login heading:** make "Welcome back" the louder line, above "Your next chapter starts here." (60px)?
11. **Library shelves:** give Currently reading, To read, DNF and Rejected the Read shelf's divided-row rhythm (today three row patterns across five shelves)?
12. **Disabled primary button:** use a neutral disabled fill instead of 50%-opacity coral ("Save name", "Search", "Invite" read as muddy but live)?
13. **Marketing capture width:** the brief specifies 850 CSS px at scale 2 (1700px wide). At 850px the app renders its tablet shell, with no rail and the BottomNav over the content ([library](2026-10-22/evidence/marketing-library-850.png), [profile](2026-10-22/evidence/marketing-profile-850.png)). The committed images were captured the same way at 1200px, which keeps the rail and matches the existing 2400px `next/image` dimensions and `docs/frontend.md`. Switch to the 850px captures?

## Not fixed

### Blocking

None left open.

### Major

- **N-L1 `/login`: no password recovery.** No "Forgot password?" link, and there is no `resetPasswordForEmail` in the codebase; `/auth/callback` already handles recovery hashes. This is a small feature rather than a design fix.
- **N-S1 server: the API hangs with the database down.** `/api/stats`, `/api/books` and `/api/recommendations` had not answered after 60s. This is why `LibraryGate` never resolves on `/swipe` in that state. It is in `lib/server`, out of scope.
- **N-S2 `/setup`: no Back and no exit.** "Add books by hand" is one-way.
- **N-S3 `/setup`: the step rail wraps** into 2 rows on desktop and 3 on phone, with connectors to nothing. It needs a phone design ("Step 3 of 6") and `<ol>` with `aria-current="step"`.
- **N-S4 `/setup`: a reload mid-enrichment lands on Upload,** despite "Safe to interrupt; it resumes".
- **N-W2 `/welcome`: the product screenshots render 350px wide on phone,** so their UI text is about 3–4px. This needs art-directed phone crops (new assets).
- **N-R4 Reveal: aversion verdicts are not said in words.** After "True", all three controls stay disabled at 50% and the only feedback is a toast ([evidence](2026-10-22/evidence/reveal-aversions-after-verdict-desktop.png)).
- **N-L2 Library detail modal: the description is a raw wall.** Markdown asterisks and a block of press quotes push the shelf actions below about 1,000px of blurb ([after](2026-10-22/after/library-detail-modal-desktop.png)).
- **N-P1 Profile: raw catalog subjects reach the reader** ("form:novel", "genre:fantasy", "Award:Hugo_Award=…"), and the genre breakdown runs to 26 rows ([profile](2026-10-22/before/profile-desktop.png)).
- **N-P2 Profile: load failure is permanent skeletons** (`TasteHero`/`ProfileView` check loading first). The same pattern remains in the Admin tabs.
- **N-P3 Profile: trait actions, evidence chips, axis labels and "Replay my reveal" are 12px;** the archetype code line is 10px.
- **N-ST1 Settings: the page looks usable when the API is down.** Forms stay editable, "Not set" disappears, and the sections say "Loading…" indefinitely.
- **N-X1 Touch targets under 44px are systemic.** `Button` md is 36px; there are 16px-tall text links ("Manage goals", "What is this?", "why", "Replay my reveal") and 24px chips.
- **N-X2 Thin focus rings:** about 50 `focus-visible:ring-1` remain (favorite hearts, profile links, trait controls). They are visible, but thinner than the 2px used elsewhere.

### Minor

- **Welcome:**
  - text below 13px in captions, the footer and `ResolveArtifact`;
  - duplicate `id="website"` honeypot across the two forms;
  - the header "Sign in" is 38px tall;
  - the artifact's ★★★★★ has no screen-reader value;
  - the two invite forms keep independent success state.
- **Login:**
  - an h2 renders before the h1;
  - the logo is not aligned with the content column on desktop;
  - 12px footer and invite line;
  - raw Supabase error text, including local-mode developer copy.
- **Setup:**
  - the progress bar has no `progressbar` semantics and repeats the percentage;
  - "Drop your CSV" copy on touch devices;
  - "you just imported" copy on the manual path;
  - 12px template instruction.
- **Reveal:**
  - from setup, "Skip to my profile" goes to `/`;
  - faux-bold mono axis letter (JetBrains Mono loads 400/500 only);
  - the tagline's "--" comes from `lib/server/archetype.ts` (out of scope).
- **Home:** "Set a reading goal in Settings." is not a link; author lines are 12px.
- **Library:**
  - the Filters popover ignores Escape and outside clicks;
  - the stretched "reading" badge;
  - shelf action chips are about 28px tall;
  - "Mark finished" is styled two ways;
  - `/to-read` flashes blank before its client redirect.
- **Discover:** the error line has no Retry.
- **Swipe:**
  - jargon ("Run a batch", "No note from the reranker");
  - the empty deck's only action is "Back to home".
- **Profile:** jargon ("Run enrich", "Re-derive"); the distribution shows 5 rows when empty and 8 when populated.
- **Settings:**
  - the goal select is 27px tall and the target input has no visible label;
  - Download buttons have no edge;
  - the uneven two-column grid.
- **Admin:**
  - `Card className="p-0"` loses to `p-5`, doubling the padding;
  - the error state is a spinner;
  - "Sync from Supabase" is shown in local mode;
  - the System tab is clipped on phone.
- **ScreenSprite:**
  - `/screen` with no titles never mentions import;
  - `/screen/profile` keeps book copy ("Reader type", "Favorite authors", "short books");
  - the Both/Movies/TV control is 32px.
- **Spacing:** off-scale values in `globals.css` (`30px 20px 22px` rail padding, 36px, 28px, 88px); `mt-7` and `mt-9` mixed on Home.
- **Swipe card (unverified):** `SwipeCard.tsx:65` back-card scale animates regardless of reduced motion (no `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`). It could not be exercised without a populated deck.

### Judged not to be findings

- Assessment A1 flagged `text-base` in `Button` `lg` and in the reveal as a colour-only class. Tailwind keeps its default `fontSize.base`, so `text-base` emits both the 16px size and the `--bg` colour, and `RevealButton` relies on the colour. No change.
- The `broken-image` and `overused-font` detector rules (see Detector).

## Per-surface notes

These summarise the critiques; the full findings are listed above.

- **`/welcome`:**
  - _Strengths:_ the invite form is a working action in the first viewport at both widths; `ResolveArtifact` is real product proof with a correct reduced-motion design; the copy is honest; there is no email address on the page.
  - _Weakness:_ the hero is a split layout with three tinted blocks that any reading app could use, while the unique proof sits fourth ([desktop](2026-10-22/before/welcome-desktop.png), [phone](2026-10-22/before/welcome-phone.png)).
- **`/login`:** a designed phone layout (the story paragraph drops and the form is above the fold), and clear focus. The two headlines compete ([desktop](2026-10-22/before/login-desktop.png), [phone](2026-10-22/before/login-phone.png)).
- **`/setup`:** warm, book-specific voice; three ingest routes visible; duration given in words. The structure is a generic stepper, and it shows the clearest "shrunk, not designed" moment, the wrapping step rail ([phone](2026-10-22/before/setup-phone.png)).
- **Reveal:** the most authored surface. The code builds up letter by letter into the sprite in the reader's own accent, the accents are contrast-solved, and the copy has character. Its faults were structural (F1), not stylistic.
- **Shell:** the rail and "Your reading room" / "Your screening room" swap are a real signature move; skip link, Escape and focus return all work.
- **Home:** reads as a reading room, not a dashboard; recommendation blocks are explained in words with a link to the fix ([desktop](2026-10-22/before/home-desktop.png), [phone](2026-10-22/before/home-phone.png), [empty](2026-10-22/states/empty-home-desktop.png)).
- **Library:** the Read shelf is the strongest screen in the app. The other shelves thin the identity with three different row patterns ([reading](2026-10-22/before/library-reading-desktop.png), [to read](2026-10-22/before/library-to-read-desktop.png), [DNF](2026-10-22/before/library-dnf-desktop.png), [edit modal](2026-10-22/before/library-edit-modal-desktop.png), [fix match](2026-10-22/before/library-correction-modal-desktop.png), [empty](2026-10-22/states/empty-library-phone.png)).
- **Discover:** a specific promise ("Every result comes from a real book catalog"); example prompts fill the field rather than auto-submitting ([phone](2026-10-22/before/discover-phone.png)).
- **Swipe:** charming sprite empty and finished states; every gesture has a labelled button ([empty](2026-10-22/before/swipe-desktop.png)).
- **Profile:** the most distinctive app page, let down by density and raw catalog data ([desktop](2026-10-22/before/profile-desktop.png), [phone](2026-10-22/before/profile-phone.png)).
- **Settings and Admin:** competent and on-palette; they could belong to any app, which is acceptable for these surfaces ([settings](2026-10-22/before/settings-desktop.png), [admin](2026-10-22/before/admin-desktop.png)).
- **ScreenSprite:** a sibling product, not a re-skin; the empty library is designed in words ([screen](2026-10-22/before/screen-desktop.png), [library](2026-10-22/before/screen-library-phone.png), [profile](2026-10-22/before/screen-profile-desktop.png)).
